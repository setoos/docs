---
title: Calling Guardrails
description: Concurrency, call-hour, and DND protections
---

Agentic OS applies guardrails automatically to keep outbound campaigns compliant
and within plan limits:

- **Concurrency cap** — respects your plan's max concurrent calls
- **Call-hour windows** — calls are only placed within the configured `callHours`
- **DND / opt-out lists** — numbers that opt out are automatically excluded from
  future campaigns
- **Per-number attempt cap** — prevents repeated same-day retries to one number

Configure call-hour windows per campaign under `schedule.callHours` when calling
[Create campaign](/api-reference/campaigns/create-campaign).
