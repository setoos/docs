---
title: Calling Guardrails
description: Concurrency, call-hour, and DND protections
---

Agentic OS applies a concurrency cap automatically — campaigns respect your
plan's max concurrent calls.

<Callout type="warning">
  Call-hour windows, DND/opt-out lists, and per-number attempt caps are not
  independently verified in this documentation set — the confirmed
  [Create campaign](/api-reference/campaigns/create-campaign) body doesn't
  include a `schedule` field. Confirm current guardrail behavior against your
  own environment before relying on it.
</Callout>
