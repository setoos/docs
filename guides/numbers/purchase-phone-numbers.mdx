---
title: Purchase Phone Numbers
description: Buy a number through your telephony provider
---

Numbers are purchased through your connected telephony provider (Plivo, Twilio, or
Acefone), then registered with Agentic OS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a provider">
    Add credentials under **Integrations** in the dashboard, or via
    `POST /integrations`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a number">
    Filter by country and area code in **Phone Numbers → Buy New Number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase">
    Confirm the purchase — it's billed directly by your provider, not Agentic OS.
  </Step>
</Steps>
