---
title: Choosing Providers
description: Picking LLM, voice, and telephony providers
---

| Layer | Options | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LLM | OpenAI, Google | GPT-4 Turbo balances cost/latency; Gemini for low-latency reasoning |
| Transcriber | Deepgram, Google STT | Deepgram Nova-3 recommended for lowest latency |
| Synthesizer | ElevenLabs | ElevenLabs for the most natural voices |
| Telephony | Twilio, Plivo, Acefone | Plivo often cheaper for India/APAC traffic |

Configure providers per-tenant under **Integrations** in the dashboard.
