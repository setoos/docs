---
title: Update contact
api: "PATCH /contact-lists/{listId}/contacts/{id}"
description: Modify phone number, name, email, or metadata of a contact
---

Updates an existing contact record in a contact list.

### Path parameters

<ParamField path="listId" type="string" required>
  The unique UUID of the contact list.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
  The unique UUID of the contact to update.
</ParamField>

### Body parameters

<ParamField body="phoneNumber" type="string">
  Updated E.164 phone number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="name" type="string">
  Updated contact name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="email" type="string">
  Updated contact email address.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="metadata" type="object">
  Updated arbitrary metadata object.
</ParamField>
