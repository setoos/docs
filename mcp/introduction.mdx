The 20X MCP server connects an AI assistant directly to your 20X account over the
[Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io). Once connected, the
assistant can build agents, assemble contact lists, run campaigns, read call
outcomes and place calls — from a chat window, with no dashboard tab open.

It is hosted by us. There is nothing to install, clone or run locally.

## The endpoint

```
https://mcp.20x.business/mcp
```

Paste that into your assistant, sign in, approve what it may do, and you are
connected. There is no key to create, copy or later rotate.

<Callout type="info">
  Use the full URL including the `/mcp` path. Some clients compare the URL you
  typed against the server's own identity and refuse to connect on a mismatch, so
  `https://mcp.20x.business` on its own will not work.
</Callout>

## What an assistant can do

64 tools, grouped by the part of the platform they touch.

| Area | Tools | What it covers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agents** | 6 | List, inspect, create, edit and delete agents. Draft prompts. |
| **Campaigns** | 12 | Build outbound call campaigns, then start, schedule, pause, resume or abort them. Draft campaigns can also be created. |
| **Contacts** | 6 | Create contact lists and fill them. |
| **Messaging** | 10 | WhatsApp and SMS post-call follow-up rules. |
| **Knowledge** | 9 | Create knowledge bases, add text, track processing. |
| **Numbers** | 5 | Search, buy and assign phone numbers. |
| **Catalog** | 5 | Voices, languages, models, personas, templates — the options to choose from. |
| **Reporting** | 4 | Call logs, usage, post-call analysis and transcripts. |
| **Calls** | 2 | Place a single outbound call. |
| **Account** | 5 | Profile, pricing, cost estimates, wallet balance. |

Of those 64: 33 only read, 16 create or change something, and 15 are flagged
destructive — they delete data, place real calls, or spend wallet balance. Most
clients ask you to confirm before running one.

## You decide what it may do

Connecting shows you a permission screen. Read-only permissions arrive pre-ticked;
anything that spends money, deletes data or reads what a customer said needs a
deliberate tick from you.

Some things are not on that screen at all, and cannot be granted by any means: the
assistant can never top up your wallet, buy a plan, reach your invoices,
transaction history, payment details or saved integration credentials, or manage
your API keys, users or roles.
