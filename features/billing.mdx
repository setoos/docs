---
title: Billing & Wallet
description: Prepaid wallet, recharges, and per-usage deductions
---

Agentic OS runs on a prepaid wallet — calls, messages, and other billable
usage deduct from your balance as they happen; there's no separate invoicing
step.

## Checking your balance

Your wallet balance and full transaction history (recharges and deductions)
are visible from the dashboard at any time.

## Recharging

Top up your wallet whenever your balance runs low.

## Exchange rates

If your wallet currency isn't USD, live conversion rates keep pricing shown
in the dashboard accurate.

## Setting your billing currency

Tenant admins can change the wallet's billing currency from account
settings.

<Callout type="info">
  Usage limits and how they map to wallet deductions vary by plan tier.
</Callout>
