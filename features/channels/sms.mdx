---
title: SMS
description: Text messages sent from a purchased or ported number
---

SMS is a one-way or two-way text channel, typically used for short
notifications or call follow-ups rather than open-ended conversation.

## Listing SMS-capable numbers

See which of your purchased or ported numbers support SMS from the dashboard.

## Sending an SMS

Send a one-off SMS to a contact directly from the dashboard.

## Campaigns

To send a templated SMS to many contacts at once — e.g. following up on
answered vs. unanswered calls — use an
[SMS Campaign](/features/campaigns/sms) instead of sending one at a time.

<Callout type="tip">
  SMS templates often require DLT registration (India) — see your provider's
  onboarding docs before sending at volume.
</Callout>
